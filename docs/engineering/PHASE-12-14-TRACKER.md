## Carried forward (known and accepted at closure)

Outside the scope of this work:

1. **A position is whatever the partner's device reports.** The server decides from what it holds, on its own clock, checks this booking's tracking pings against it, and writes down only what it held — and every one of those readings came from the partner's device. A client built to lie reports a consistent false position and is believed. Money no longer moves on that alone (P0-7c), and what remains are controls a person can check afterwards: the photo, the risk signal, the customer's dispute, an admin's review. Closing it needs device attestation with a mock-location signal in the mobile apps.
2. **The mobile apps do not carry the new flows.** The partner app sends photos as data URLs (accepted), but an iPhone HEIC photo is refused with "send JPEG or PNG"; it has no door-photo step and does not read the `noShow` preview, so a no-show reported from it takes no fee; it does not send `null` coordinates for a device with no location. The customer app does not know the new refusals of "professional didn't arrive". The web flows are the model for both.
3. **No deployed environment exists.** Migration `20261006190000_service_defaults_draft` is applied on the developer database and not on staging; no production target is named. The stranded-refund sweep (which now also finishes a no-show's refund after a crash) runs only where `REFUND_AUTO_RECOVERY_ENABLED=true` in production; where it is off, such a refund stays "pending" until someone acts.

Decisions recorded (owner, 2026-10-07 — "whatever is right for the project, customer- and partner-friendly"), each changeable without a design change:

4. The no-show rules in P0-7c (fewer fees are taken automatically; a disagreement goes to a person). Exception lifetime 2 hours (customer) / 24 hours (admin). Evidence limits 12 photos a stage, 40 rows a job per partner; a 90-day window for "the same photo on another job". A door photo counts from an hour before the booked time. A customer who tapped "the professional is here" can still be charged a no-show fee when the arrival was confirmed from a position and the rest of the rule holds (otherwise that tap would immunise against every fee).
5. The materials and equipment policy of the 25 live services follows the published customer copy: everyday home help — some materials are the customer's, equipment is the home's; dedicated or specialised cleaning, salon and car — the professional brings both; plant care — the customer's materials, the professional's tools. An admin can change any of them in the service editor.
6. On a live service a protection is reduced only by pause → edit → publish again (second admin), never by an edit or a revision.
