## SLIDE — WHAT IS HOMEEIGO?

**HOMEEIGO is a trusted home-services marketplace.**

We connect customers who need work done in their home with **verified, trained service
professionals** — and we run the entire operation end to end: booking, payment, matching, live
tracking, job verification, partner earnings and payouts.

**One line:** *Book a verified professional for any home service — cleaning, repair, laundry, beauty
and more — with live tracking, secure payment and privacy-protected communication.*

**Three audiences, one platform**
- **Customers** — book, pay, track, rate
- **Service Partners** — get work, execute jobs, earn, withdraw
- **Operations** — acquire partners, dispatch, reconcile money, protect trust
